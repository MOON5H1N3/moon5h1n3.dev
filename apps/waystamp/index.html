Welcome to Waystamp

A stamp book for the places you walk to.

  1. Collect stamps. Tap any place, summit, trail or sauna and pick the date you went. It's inked into your book with its own picture. Been back? Log another visit.
  2. Put it on your home screen. On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap Install app at the bottom of the page. It then works without signal.
  3. Keep a backup. Your stamps are saved on this phone only. Use Save a backup at the bottom of the page now and then, and Restore a backup on a new phone.
